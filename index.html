Frequent Asked Questions

Can I access passwords on devices?

Yes, you can securely access your saved passwords anytime across your devices with proper authentication.

Does the app generate strong passwords?

Yes, our built-in password generator creates strong, unique, and secure passwords instantly to protect your accounts.

Are my passwords stored securely here?

Yes, we use advanced end-to-end encryption to ensure your passwords and sensitive information remain completely secure and private.

What if I forget my master password?

For security reasons, your master password cannot be viewed. However, recovery options are available if backup is enabled.
